### Analysis and Redesign Strategy

The current schema follows a strict Third Normal Form (3NF) structure, typical for transactional OLTP systems. While efficient for data insertion and updates, it suffers from high join complexity for analytical queries. For example, calculating student performance requires joining `aluno_ifb`, `matricula_ifb`, `desempenho_enem`, and potentially `escola`. Aggregating school statistics requires joining `escola`, `localizacao`, and `indicadores_escolares`.

To optimize for read-heavy workloads (BI dashboards, reporting), the strategy shifts to a **Denormalized Star Schema** or a **Flat Analytical Table** approach depending on the granularity needed. Given the specific constraints of the source data (e.g., `desempenho_enem` having multiple metrics per year per school), a hybrid approach is best:

1.  **Consolidate Dimensions**: Merge `escola` and `localizacao` into a single `dim_escola` table to eliminate joins between school and location data.
2.  **Flatten Fact Tables**:
    *   **Student Lifecycle**: Merge `aluno_ifb` and `matricula_ifb` into a `fact_matricula` table where each row represents a specific enrollment event with all student attributes pre-joined.
    *   **Performance**: Flatten `desempenho_enem` to include school attributes directly.
    *   **School Metrics**: Flatten `indicadores_escolares` to include location and school name.
    *   **Employment**: Flatten `vinculo_empregaticio` to include student, salary, and location attributes.
3.  **Remove Redundant Joins**: By embedding foreign keys and attributes directly into the fact tables, queries can retrieve complex reports in a single `SELECT` statement without multiple `JOIN`s.
