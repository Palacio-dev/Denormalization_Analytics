We will consolidate all data into a single table named `BigDataModel`.
- **Primary Key**: A composite key `(EntityId, EntityType)` where `EntityType` is a string enum (e.g., 'Album', 'Artist', 'Track', 'Customer', 'Invoice', 'Employee', 'Genre', 'MediaType', 'Playlist', 'PlaylistTrack').
- **Foreign Keys**: Removed. All relationships are resolved via the `EntityType` column and the inclusion of all relevant attributes in the single row.
- **Attribute Handling**:
    - Attributes from `Artist` (Name, City, etc.) will be added to the `Artist` rows.
    - Attributes from `Album` (Title, City, etc.) will be added to the `Album` rows.
    - Attributes from `Track` (Name, Composer, etc.) will be added to the `Track` rows.
    - *Note*: `Customer` and `Invoice` tables are kept separate in terms of business logic but physically merged into the big table because they don't have a clear "parent-child" relationship with the music domain in the same way; however, to strictly follow "One Big Table", they are included with their own `EntityType`.
    - `Playlist` and `PlaylistTrack` are also included.
